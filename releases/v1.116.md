## v1.116 – Collect logs: lost pieces are sent again, nothing goes missing silently

Until now a diagnostics log with one lost network packet was dropped without any message.

- **Retransmit:** the Overview asks the lamp again for the pieces it misses (after 1.5 s without a new piece, up to 4 times); the lamp keeps the log it sent for 2 minutes for that.
- **One line per lamp in the event log:** *diagnostics log received* · *no diagnostics log on that PC* (Diagnostics was never on) · *diagnostics log incomplete (n of m pieces arrived)* · *no diagnostics log received* (no answer within 20 s – lamp older than v1.114, or its messages do not reach the master PC).
- Copies arriving over a second network (LAN + ZeroTier) are ignored; piece numbers are checked; at most 32 logs are received at once.
