### Change in v1.45 – countdown on the calling PC too
The PC that pressed **F12** now also shows the call's countdown, like the receiving PCs:

- on the lamp: `F12 · 18 s` (when nobody on the band is transmitting),
- on the line under it: `F12 call ON (7 MHz) · 18 s left – F12 = off` (also along the bottom of the lamp in compact mode).

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
