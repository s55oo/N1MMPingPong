## v1.114 – diagnostics: collect logs, rotation, DIAG mark, contest checklist

- **Collect logs from all lamps** – Overview → **Settings ▾ → Collect diagnostics logs from all lamps**. Every lamp sends the newest ~600 KB of its `pingpong_test.log` (compressed, signed with the network password); the Overview saves each as `logs\<name>_<date>_<time>.log` next to its exe and notes it in the event log. Master PC only, like the other commands.
- **Diagnostics state** – a red **DIAG** mark in the lamp's top bar while Diagnostics is on; the Overview menu shows *Diagnostics ON on all lamps (now on: n of m)*.
- **Log rotation** – a `pingpong_test.log` bigger than 5 MB is rotated (`.1`, `.2`, `.3`; the oldest is deleted) when Diagnostics is switched on and while it runs.
- **README** – new section *4. Contest checklist*; Diagnostics section updated.
- 7 new tests.

Update: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview. Old lamps ignore the new messages, so update all PCs before collecting logs.
