### Fix – lamp now works on SSB
N1MM reports phone mode as **`SSB`** in its RadioInfo packets, but the lamp only accepted `USB`/`LSB`. So on SSB the lamp never lit up, even while transmitting. SSB, USB and LSB now all count as one **SSB** group (footer shows e.g. `spremljam 14 SSB`).

Found with the v1.10 test-mode log.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp and answer **Yes**, or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**.
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
