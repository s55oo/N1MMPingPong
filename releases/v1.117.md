## v1.117 – BAND VIOLATION alert from N1MM_timer

PingPong now shows the band-change alerts of [N1MM_timer](https://github.com/s55oo/N1MM_timer) (a separate program on the master PC that checks the 10-minute rule, hourly band changes and MULT on the RUN band). PingPong itself checks no rules – it only displays and relays.

- **Red footer** on every lamp, in the Overview and on its web page: `BAND VIOLATION: <text> (+N more)`, plus `· YOUR STATION` on the PC whose N1MM StationName logged it. One beep per new violation (if the lamp's sound is on – the option is now called *Sound (F12 calls, BAND VIOLATION)*).
- **Grey `Band watcher offline`** when N1MM_timer was heard but has been silent for longer than its `ttl_s`; cleared when it reports `ok`.
- N1MM_timer → UDP **127.0.0.1:46071** (loopback only) → the master's lamp → all lamps and the Overview over the signed lamp network (46070). Accepted only from the master PC. Port: `[band_alert] port` in `pingpong.ini` (`0` = off).
- Advisory only: never touches the ping-pong lock, F-keys, radios or N1MM. Older lamps ignore it.
- Test without N1MM_timer: `python tools/send_band_alert.py` on the master PC (`--stop` for the offline state, `--station NAME` for YOUR STATION).
- 24 new tests (271 in total). Merged from PR #4.

Not yet run on the station. Previous version: v1.116 (Options ▾ → Updates → Revert).

Update: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview.
