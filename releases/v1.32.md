### New in v1.32 – frequency on the lamp, compact-mode fix
- The lamp's **top-right corner shows the frequency of the transmitting station in kHz**, e.g. `14025.00 kHz` (from N1MM's RadioInfo). During an F12 call it shows the caller's frequency.
- **Fix:** in compact mode (Shift+F) the computer name and the status line were hidden under the wide call border. They are now drawn inside it.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
