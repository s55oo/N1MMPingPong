## v1.106 – reverse CW counts as CW

N1MM reports reverse CW (CW on the upper/lower sideband) as `CW-R`. PingPong did not know that mode, so one PC on `CW-R` and another on `CW` were not in the same group and their lamps did not show each other's messages.

- `CW-R`, `CW-U`, `CW-L`, `CWR` now count as **CW**; `FSK`, `FSK-R` as **RTTY** (like `RTTY-R` since v1.100). Case is ignored.
