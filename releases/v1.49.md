## PingPong v1.49

**F12 lock only for S&P**
- While another PC's F12 call runs on your band, F12 is ignored **only if your station is in S&P**, i.e. the stations the call is aimed at. Your lamp shows `BLOCKED · F12`, and the line under it says `F12 ignored – RUN1 is calling on 14 MHz`.
- A station in **Run** can always press F12 and start its own call, as before v1.48.
- Ending your own call with F12 always works.

Update all PCs.
