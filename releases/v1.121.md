## Silent CQ WW band warnings

Multi-Two shows one change left at 7/8 and a steady amber banner at 8/8.
Each further band change flashes red for ten seconds on the desktop and phone
lamps. Multi-Single uses the same warning for early RUN or MULT changes and
keeps the original legal band and deadline. The MULT timer starts with a new
zone or country. Assign a local radio’s RUN or MULT role under Options → Display
to see amber when tuning away during its hold.

Upgrade the master and every lamp to v1.121 or later. The master derives the
warnings directly from its synchronized N1MM log.
