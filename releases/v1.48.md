## PingPong v1.48

**One F12 call per band**
- While another PC's F12 call is running on your band, **F12 on your PC is ignored**. This applies in Run or S&P, and also if you silenced that call. No second call starts.
- The lamp briefly shows `BLOCKED · F12`, or `F12 busy · RUN1` on a lamp that isn't blinking. The line under it says `F12 ignored – RUN1 is calling on 14 MHz`.
- When that call ends (F12 on the caller, or the F12 block time is over), F12 works again.
- Ending your own call with F12 always works.
- N1MM still receives the key, as before.

Update all PCs.
