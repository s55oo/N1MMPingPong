## PingPong v1.76

**The lamp now listens for its own N1MM on UDP 12062 (was 12060)**

⚠ **Do this first, on every N1MM PC, before updating the lamps.** In N1MM: **Configurer → External Broadcast → Broadcast Address**, add `127.0.0.1:12062`. To keep other programs (e.g. Callbooker) on 12060, enter both, separated by a space:

```
127.0.0.1:12060 127.0.0.1:12062
```

- The lamp no longer shares N1MM's usual port with other programs, so nothing can take its N1MM packets away any more.
- If a lamp hears nothing from its own N1MM for 30 s, the line under it says: `no data from this PC's N1MM on UDP 12062 – N1MM Broadcast Address must include 127.0.0.1:12062`. The Overview's ready check shows `no N1MM data on UDP 12062` in red.
- **Allow PingPong in Windows Firewall…** now opens UDP 12062 and 46070.
- A different port is still possible with `n1mm_lamps.exe --port <n>`.

Then: **Update all lamps…** in the Overview; the Overview updates itself.
