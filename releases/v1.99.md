## PingPong v1.99

**A nicer-looking lamp**

Only the look changed – everything works as before.

- **Dark window:** dark background, light status text, and a dark title bar on Windows 10/11.
- **Rounded lamp:** the lamp has rounded corners; while an F12 call border is lit, the border's inner corners are rounded too.
- **Options ▾** is a small button that lights up under the mouse.
- **Warnings** under the lamp are a lighter red that reads well on the dark window; the phone address is a quiet grey.
- Lamp colors, texts, sizes, night mode and compact mode are unchanged.
- The release also carries the captain's quick chart, `PingPong-captain-chart.pdf`.
