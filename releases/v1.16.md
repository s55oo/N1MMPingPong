### New in v1.16 – F-key block ends by itself after 40 s
If the caller forgets to press F12 again, the F-key block on the other PCs ends by itself **40 seconds** after the call started. The lamp keeps blinking (footer: `blokada potekla`) until the caller ends the call or you click the lamp. While blocked, the footer counts down: `F-tipke blokirane še 35 s`.

(v1.15: only N1MM's function keys **F1–F11** are blocked; Enter, Esc and all other keys always work.)

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
