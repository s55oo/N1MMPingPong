### Change in v1.37 – who is transmitting, at a glance
While someone transmits, the line under the lamp now shows **name (hostname) · RUN/INB · transmitting · band mode**, e.g.

`INB1 (OO840G3) · INB · transmitting · 14 CW`

- **RUN / INB** is the station's ping-pong role if set, otherwise from N1MM's mode: **Run = RUN, S&P = INB**.
- The hostname (Windows computer name) is shown when it differs from the station's name.
- The station list uses the same words: `RUN1 14 CW RUN (me) · INB1 14 CW INB`.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older** (title shows e.g. `v1.0`): download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
