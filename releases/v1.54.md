## PingPong v1.54

**Warning: another program on UDP 12060**
- If another program on the PC (e.g. **Callbooker**) also listens on N1MM's port, the line under the lamp says, in red: `⚠ UDP 12060 also used by Callbooker.exe – N1MM data may be missing here`.
- When two programs listen on one port, Windows gives each N1MM packet sent to `127.0.0.1` to only one of them. The lamp, and the other PCs' lamps, then miss this PC's N1MM.
- **Fix:** in N1MM's Broadcast Address, enter two destinations separated by a space, e.g. `127.0.0.1:12060 127.0.0.1:12061`. Then set the other program to 12061. See the README section *Another program on 12060*.
- Checked every 30 s.

**More robust**
- An unexpected error can no longer stop the lamp's display or its messages to the other lamps. It is written to `pingpong_error.log`, and the lamp carries on.

Update all PCs.
