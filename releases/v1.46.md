### Change in v1.46 – MASTER in the title bar
The title bar no longer shows `UDP 12060`. Instead the **master's** lamp shows `PingPong  -  MASTER  v1.46`; all other lamps show just `PingPong  v1.46`. It changes by itself when the master changes in N1MM.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
