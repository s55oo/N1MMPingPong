### Change in v1.33
The **computer name** of the transmitting station is now drawn **on the lamp's top-left corner**, opposite the frequency on the top-right – in the normal window too. It is no longer in the gray row above the lamp.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
