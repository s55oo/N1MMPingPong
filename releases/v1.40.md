### Change in v1.40 – better diagnostics
- **Diagnostics** now also logs every N1MM `RadioInfo` from **other PCs**, marked `(direct)` or `(relayed)`, with station name and Run/S&P.
- Each lamp reports **which stations it sees** and whether it shares its N1MM – visible in the other PCs' Diagnostics log.
- Unexpected errors in the lamp's network part are written to `pingpong_error.log` and the lamp keeps running (before, such an error could silently stop it from hearing other lamps).

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
