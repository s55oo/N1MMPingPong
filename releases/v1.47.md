## PingPong v1.47

**Countdowns**
- **F12 call:** the blinking lamps on the S&P stations now count down too, e.g. `F12 · RUN1 · 18 s`. The line under the lamp shows `… · 18 s left`.
- **Ping-pong lock:** a locked INB shows who holds the slot and when the lock releases itself, e.g. `WAIT · INB1 · 32 s`, with `slot taken by INB1 – wait for RUN (free in 32 s at the latest)` under it.

**Version warning**
- The line under the lamp warns when another PC runs a different PingPong: `RUN2: v1.45 – update it`, or `RUN2: v1.48 – update this PC`.

**Firewall help**
- If N1MM data from a PC arrives but no other lamp is heard at all, the line under the lamp says `PC5: no PingPong heard – firewall?`. If other lamps are heard, it says `PC5: no PingPong lamp there`. Both notes appear 30 s after start.
- New: **Options ▾ → Allow PingPong in Windows Firewall…** adds the inbound rule `PingPong lamps UDP` for UDP 12060 and 46070 (private/domain networks). Windows asks for administrator rights.

Update all PCs.
