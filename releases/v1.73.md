## PingPong v1.73

**Overview: network round-trip time (ping)**
- Every **5 minutes**, and at once with **Ping now** on the Activity tab, the Overview pings every lamp over PingPong's own protocol. Each lamp answers at once, and the Overview measures the **round-trip time in ms**.
- **Ping** column on the Stations tab, e.g. `3.2 ms`, or `lost`.
- **Ping avg / max / lost** per station on the Activity tab.
- **Ready check:** `no ping reply`, or `slow network` (over 500 ms), turns the row red.
- Lost and slow pings are written to the event log.
- Lamps answer only the master PC, from v1.73 on.

Update: **Update all lamps…** in the Overview; the Overview updates itself.
