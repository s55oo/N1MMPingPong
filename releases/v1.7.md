### New in v1.7 – choose your color
**Options ▾ → My color…** – each PC chooses the color of its own station, and every PingPong lamp in the network shows that station in that color.

- Colors already chosen by other PCs are greyed out with the PC's name, so the same color can't be picked twice.
- If two PCs still end up with the same color (one was offline when the other chose), the one that chose **first** keeps it; the other shows `barva … je zasedena` and uses an automatic color until you pick another.
- **Automatic** clears the choice. Stations without a chosen color keep getting palette colors, but never one another PC has chosen.
- Saved per PC in `pingpong.ini`.

### Updating
- **From v1.6:** start the lamp and answer **Yes** to the update question, or use **Options ▾ → Check for updates**.
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.

Update all PCs – only v1.7+ lamps exchange colors. See the [README](https://github.com/s55oo/N1MMPingPong/blob/main/README.md#my-color-options-menu-v17) for details.
