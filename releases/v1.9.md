### Important fix – please update all PCs
**v1.5–v1.8 could make N1MM Logger+ show an error** (`IndexOutOfRangeException` in `MultiOpManager.HandleUDPMessage`). PingPong's discovery messages used UDP port 12070, which N1MM's own multi-computer networking also listens on.

v1.9 moves PingPong discovery to UDP port **46070**, away from N1MM's ports. Nothing else changes; your color choice and settings are kept.

### Updating
- **From v1.6–v1.8:** start the lamp and answer **Yes**, or use **Options ▾ → Check for updates**.
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.

Until every PC is updated, any lamp still on v1.5–v1.8 keeps sending on 12070. Close those lamps, or update them first.
