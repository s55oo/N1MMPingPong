### New in v1.14 – block keys during an F12 call
**Options ▾ → Block my F-keys while another station calls (F12)** – turn it on on the PCs that should be stopped (saved per PC, off by default).

- While the lamp blinks for an F12 call (same band, S&P), the keys that make N1MM send are blocked: **F1–F11, Enter, Insert, `;` and `'`**. The lamp shows `BLOKIRANO · F1`.
- **Esc is never blocked** (so a transmission can always be stopped), nor Alt combinations, letters or F12.
- Blocking ends when the caller presses F12 again, or when you click the lamp, change band or go to Run.
- Works only while N1MM is the active window. It does not stop mouse clicks on N1MM's buttons, mic PTT, paddle or footswitch – a guard against accidental key presses, not a hardware interlock.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
