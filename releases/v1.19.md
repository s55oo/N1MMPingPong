### Fix – F12 call ignored between PCs with VirtualBox (or VPN) adapters
A lamp ignored calls it thought came from its own PC by comparing **all** network addresses. VirtualBox gives every PC the same host-only address (`192.168.56.1`), and some VPN adapters do the same, so a call from another PC was taken as "my own" and ignored: no blinking, no F-key blocking.

Now "same PC" means the message really came from one of this PC's own addresses or carries this PC's name.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.

Update **all** PCs.
