## PingPong v1.93

**Priority call: Ctrl+F2 / Ctrl+F4 – your message goes out alone**

- Press **Ctrl+F2** or **Ctrl+F4** in N1MM (PingPong takes the key; N1MM doesn't get Ctrl+F4).
- Every station on the **same band and mode** that is **transmitting** gets an **Esc** in N1MM from its own lamp – its message stops at once – and its **F1–F11, Enter and Insert are held** until your message has been sent (Esc and F12 always work).
- About half a second later (once Ctrl is let go) your lamp presses **F2 / F4** in N1MM for you.
- All those lamps **flash exactly like Shift+F2/F4** (your color / white, `RUN1 · QRL?`), with `PRIORITY: RUN1` in the corner and "your transmission was stopped – keys held until it is sent" under it. Your own lamp shows `PRIORITY`. The Overview shows and logs `PRIORITY` calls.
- It ends when your F2/F4 message has been sent (keys free again at the latest after 30 s). Other bands and modes are never touched. Needs **F12 call** on (Options ▾), like Shift+F2/F4.
- All lamps should be v1.93 – older lamps only flash, they don't stop.
