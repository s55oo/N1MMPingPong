### Change in v1.27 – who is sending
The top-left corner of the lamp no longer just says *PingPong*. It shows the **computer that is sending on your band right now** (e.g. `OO840G3`), `F12: OO840G3` during an F12 call, and `PingPong` when nobody is sending.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
