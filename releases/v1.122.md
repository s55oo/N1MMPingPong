## v1.122 – CQ WW counters on the lamp's lower corners

The RUN / MULT countdowns (Multi-Single) and the TX1 / TX2 band changes (Multi-Two) are now drawn **inside the coloured lamp**, on its lower corners – like the station name and frequency on the upper corners:

- bottom left: **RUN** or **TX1**, e.g. `RUN 20 m · 6:12`, `TX1 20 m · 7/8 · 1 left`
- bottom right: **MULT** or **TX2**, e.g. `MULT 15 m · clear`, `TX2 40 m · 0/8`

In compact mode (Shift+F) they sit just above the status line drawn on the lamp. Warnings (unclear log, missing feed, waiting for counts), the amber/red band banners and the optional *Last QSO* line stay where they were. The phone lamp is unchanged. 7 new tests.

Update: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview.
