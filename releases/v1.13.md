### Fixes for the F12 call
- **Only one lamp per PC.** Two lamps on the same PC saw each other as "remote" PCs, and F12 switched both at once, so one of them kept blinking. Starting a second lamp now just says PingPong is already running.
- A call from the same PC never blinks its own lamps.
- **Click a blinking lamp to silence it.** The receiving PC can now stop the blinking itself (footer: `klik = utišaj`). A new F12 call blinks again.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
