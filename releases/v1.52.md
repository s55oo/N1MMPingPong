## PingPong v1.52

**Urgent call: Shift+F4**
- Press **Shift+F4** in N1MM. N1MM still fires **F4** as usual, and at the same time every other PingPong PC on the **same band and mode**, Run or S&P, **flashes fast**:
  - lamp and border swap between your color and white;
  - the lamp shows `!! RUN1 !!` and gives three high beeps;
  - it takes over the lamp, even over a transmission or an F12 call.
- Your own lamp counts down: `URGENT · 25 s`.
- It ends with **Shift+F4 again**, by itself after **30 s**, or on one PC with a click on the lamp.
- No keys are blocked by an urgent call.
- It needs **F12 call** turned on in Options. The menu item now says *F12 call – blink stations on my band (Shift+F4 = urgent)*.

Update all PCs. Older lamps don't show urgent calls.
