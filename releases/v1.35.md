### New in v1.35
- **The lamp remembers its place and compact mode.** After a restart or an update it comes back where it was, compact (Shift+F) or not. If that place is no longer on a screen (e.g. a monitor was unplugged), it opens at the default place.
- **Sound on F12 call.** The receiving PC gives a short double beep when a call starts blinking there – once per call. **Options ▾ → Sound on F12 call** (on by default).

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
