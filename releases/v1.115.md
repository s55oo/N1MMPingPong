## v1.115 – echo filter: a lost "TX off" packet can no longer hide a PC for up to 10 s

Found in a code review (not seen on the station). Two PCs on one shared radio: the echo filter decided "the other PC is still transmitting" with the display timeout `--stale` (15 s). If the other PC's "TX off" packet was lost on the network, this PC's TX packets without caption were ignored as echoes until N1MM's next status packet – up to 10 s.

- The echo filter now has its own limit: the other PC counts as transmitting only for **3 s** after its last packet. `--stale` (15 s, from v1.101) is unchanged and still right for the display.
- Why 3 s: in the station logs of 14–15 Nov all 55 echoes came at most 1.6 s after the other PC's last packet.
- Trade-off: an echo more than 3 s into a long message of the other PC (with no packet from it in between) is shown again as red without a message. Not seen in the logs.
- 3 new tests; `docs/STATUS-shared-radio.md` and the README describe it, with the open points of the review.

Not yet confirmed on the station. Previous version: v1.114 (Options ▾ → Updates → Revert).
