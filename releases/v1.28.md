### Change in v1.28
The top-left corner of the lamp is now **empty when nobody is sending** (the window title already says PingPong). It shows the sending computer, e.g. `OO840G3`, and `F12: OO840G3` during an F12 call.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
