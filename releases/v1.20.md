### Change in v1.20 – Test mode becomes Diagnostics
**Options ▾ → Diagnostics (log to file)** replaces *Test mode*. It only writes the log (`pingpong_test.log`: N1MM packets, other lamps' state, F12 presses, blocked keys) and no longer changes what the lamp shows. Off by default, always off after a restart. It sits at the bottom of the Options menu now.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
