## v1.102 – one shared radio for several PCs

When the N1MMs of two PCs share **one** radio (e.g. N1MM_vRadio2), both report the radio's transmission, so the lamp showed the wrong PC or flipped between them (RUN2 / RUN3).

- PingPong now gives the transmission to **one** PC per frequency: the first to report TX (a PC whose packet carries a message caption wins over one without).
- The other PC's TX flag is ignored while the owner transmits, and for **1.75 s** after its last TX (PTT tail + break-in delay).
- Only when two PCs report TX on exactly the same frequency; separate radios, also on the same band, are untouched. Nothing to set. Works in the lamp and in the Overview statistics.
- 7 new tests.

Update the lamps **and** the Overview: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview.
