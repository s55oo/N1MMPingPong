## v1.104 – N1MM_vRadio2 decides who transmits

For PCs that share one radio through **N1MM_vRadio2 1.0.9+** (`pcs = 2`), PingPong now uses vRadio's own `vRadioShare` packet (UDP 46070) instead of guessing from N1MM's `IsTransmitting`:

- PCs are paired by IP (`Ips` = host, `Clients` = clients). Only the PC that vRadio says keys (`Tx` / `TxFrom`) counts as transmitting; the other's flag is ignored – also on different VFOs and frequencies.
- The lamp and the Overview re-judge at once when vRadio's packet changes, so it does not matter which packet arrives first.
- No packet for 3 s → pairing dropped, N1MM's flag is used again. PCs not in a share are unchanged. The packet is not part of the network password.
- The frequency-based rule of v1.102/v1.103 stays for other ways of sharing a radio.
- 11 new tests.

Update the lamps **and** the Overview: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview. vRadio must be 1.0.9 or newer with `pcs = 2`.
