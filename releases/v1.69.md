## PingPong v1.69

**Lamp**
- **GO for the inband stations:** when RUN's transmission ends and the slot is free, every inband lamp shows a green **GO** for 3 s. That's the moment to transmit. It disappears as soon as an inband station takes the slot, and the others then show `WAIT`.
- **Options ▾ → Sound on GO** adds a short soft click. It is off by default.
- **Warning when ping-pong can't work:** an inband lamp says `ping-pong: no RUN on 7 MHz – lock off`, e.g. when the run station is in S&P by mistake.
- The lamp now reports its own problems to the Overview:
  - another program on UDP 12060 (e.g. Callbooker);
  - keyboard watcher not running;
  - errors in `pingpong_error.log`;
  - no RUN on its band.

**Overview**
- **Check column / ready check:** those lamp problems show in red, so you see them from the master PC.
- **New Activity tab:** per station, how often and how long it waited (ping-pong `WAIT`), F12 and urgent calls, total TX time, and a chart of **TX minutes per hour**. Also on the web page and in **Save CSV…**.

Update: **Update all lamps…** in the Overview; the Overview updates itself.
