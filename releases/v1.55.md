## PingPong v1.55

**Urgent call (Shift+F4) follows the F4 message**
- The fast flashing on the other stations now lasts **exactly while your F4 message is being sent**. It stops as soon as N1MM stops transmitting, whether the message is done or you pressed Esc.
- If N1MM starts no transmission within 3 s (e.g. F4 has no message), the urgent call ends by itself. It never lasts longer than 30 s.
- Shift+F4 no longer switches the call off. Each Shift+F4 starts a new urgent call with that F4 message.
- A click on a flashing lamp still silences it on that PC.

Update all PCs.
