## PingPong v1.89

**Overview: reset the statistics at the contest start – automatically**

- New **Statistics ▾** button (second row):
  - **Reset statistics now…**
  - **Reset at contest start…** – proposes the **next Saturday 01:00 local** (CQ WW: Saturday 00:00 UTC = 01:00 CET); any date and time can be typed, e.g. `2026-11-28 01:00`.
  - **Cancel planned reset**, **Contest report…**, **Save CSV…**
- While a reset is planned the button is yellow – `Statistics ▾ (reset Sat 28.11. 01:00)` – and the bottom line counts down. At that moment the function key and activity counters go to zero by themselves (event log: `statistics reset · planned for …`).
- The plan survives a restart of the Overview; if it isn't running at that time, it resets when it starts (only statistics from before the planned time).
