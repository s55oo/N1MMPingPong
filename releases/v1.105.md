## v1.105 – Diagnostics for vRadio sharing

With **Options ▾ → Diagnostics (log to file)** on, the lamp now writes every change of vRadio's `vRadioShare` packet to `pingpong_test.log`: Tx, TxFrom, the host and client addresses, what it means for each PC (`True` = transmits, `False` = does not, `None` = not paired), and this PC's own addresses. This shows at once whether the pairing by IP address works.
