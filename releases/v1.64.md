## PingPong v1.64

**Ping-pong role: always from N1MM**
- The role always follows N1MM: **Run = RUN**, **S&P = INB**. The fixed choices (*always RUN*, *always INB*, *off*) are gone from Options. The menu only shows the current role.
- An old fixed role in `pingpong.ini` is ignored.
- The keyboard watcher now always runs, because the ping-pong lock may need it whenever N1MM is in S&P.
