## PingPong v1.90

**SHA-256 checksums with every release**

- Every release now carries **`SHA256SUMS.txt`** with the SHA-256 of `n1mm_lamps.exe`, `pingpong_overview.exe` and the cheat sheet PDF; the same hashes are at the end of these notes.
- The lamp and Overview updaters check each download against GitHub's own record **and** against `SHA256SUMS.txt` – if either doesn't match, or the two disagree, nothing is installed.
- Check a download by hand: `Get-FileHash .\n1mm_lamps.exe` in PowerShell (or `certutil -hashfile n1mm_lamps.exe SHA256`) and compare.
