## PingPong v1.56

**Urgent call (Shift+F4)**
- The flashing lamps now show the **caller's name and the F4 message** being sent, e.g. `RUN1 · QRL?`. They show `RUN1 · MSG` until N1MM reports the caption.
- **No time limit any more.** The urgent call lasts exactly while the F4 message is transmitted. It still ends after 3 s if no transmission starts (e.g. F4 has no message). A calling lamp that disappears is dropped after 12 s, like any lamp.

Update all PCs.
