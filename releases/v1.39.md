### New in v1.39 – lamps share N1MM data on every network
Each lamp now passes **its own N1MM's RadioInfo** on to the other lamps, over the same connection the lamps already use (UDP 46070) – on **every network** the PCs share: LAN, Wi-Fi, **ZeroTier**, other VPNs.

- N1MM's **Broadcast Data** can simply be `127.0.0.1:12060` on every PC – no LAN/VPN broadcast addresses to keep in sync.
- Stations reachable only over ZeroTier (or a VPN) now show on the lamps.
- If N1MM's own broadcast also arrives (same LAN), the lamp uses that and ignores the copy; each PC is listed once.
- **Options ▾ → Share my N1MM with other lamps (all networks)** – on by default.

Every PC whose station should be seen needs v1.39 (its lamp does the sharing).

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
