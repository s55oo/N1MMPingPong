## v1.108 – echo filter for two PCs on one shared radio

Based on a real capture of RUN2 and RUN3 (two PCs, one radio through a CAT translator). Each PC's N1MM reports its own transmission with the key's caption (`F4 S51CAB`). But RUN2's N1MM also reported `IsTransmitting=True` **without a caption** right after RUN3's transmission – 116 of its 270 TX packets – so the lamp showed RUN2 red without a message (and the lock could count it).

- A TX packet **without caption** is ignored when another PC on the same band and mode is transmitting, or sent a captioned TX later (within 1.5 s): an echo of the shared radio.
- A TX packet without caption right after the **same** PC's own captioned TX (within 1.5 s) is the same message going on: its caption is used again.
- Everything else is left as N1MM reports it. Only N1MM's `RadioInfo` is used – nothing from vRadio. Works in the lamp and in the Overview statistics.
- Replayed on the real log: RUN2 transmissions shown without a message 99 → 6; RUN3 unchanged. 8 new tests (with real packets).

Not yet confirmed on the station. If it misbehaves: v1.107 is the previous version (Options ▾ → Updates → Revert).

Update: Overview → **Update all lamps…**, then restart the Overview.
