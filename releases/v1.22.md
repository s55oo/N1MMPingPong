### Change in v1.22 – wider, bolder F12 call border
- **Caller:** while your F12 call is on, your lamp has a **10 px border in your color that blinks** (was a thin, steady 4 px line).
- **Receiving PCs:** besides the blinking lamp, a **10 px border in the caller's color** stays lit, also in the dark phase of the blink.
- The border is invisible when there is no call; the window no longer changes size when a call starts or ends.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
