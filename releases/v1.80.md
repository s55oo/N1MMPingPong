## PingPong v1.80

**Lamp shows its N1MM port**

- The lamp's title bar shows the UDP port it listens on for N1MM, in front of the version: `PingPong - MASTER UDP 12062 v1.80` on the master, `PingPong UDP 12062 v1.80` on the others. The same line is at the bottom of **Options ▾**.
- So you can see at a glance that the lamp listens where N1MM's **Broadcast Data → Radio** line sends (`127.0.0.1:12062`), also when a lamp is started with `--port`.
