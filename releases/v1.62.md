## PingPong v1.62

**Overview: one row per PC after a lamp update**
- When a lamp restarts, e.g. after **Update all lamps…**, the Overview used to show that PC twice for up to 2 minutes: once green, and once dark red with "not heard". Now the restarted lamp replaces its old row at once, and its last transmission is kept.

This is also the first release the Overview v1.61 can **update by itself**: start it and answer **Yes**, or click **Update Overview to v1.62…**. Lamps on v1.60 or newer: **Update all lamps…**.
