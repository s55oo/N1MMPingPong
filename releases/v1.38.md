### Change in v1.38
**Compact mode** is now also in the menu: **Options ▾ → Compact mode** (shortcut **Shift+F** shown next to it). The tick shows whether compact mode is on; in compact mode, right-click the lamp to open the menu and untick it.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5 or older:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one once.
