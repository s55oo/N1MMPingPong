### New in v1.31 – compact mode (Shift+F)
Click the lamp and press **Shift+F**: no title bar, no top row, no gray margins – only the lamp, same size and place. The sending PC's name appears small in the lamp's top-left corner and the status line along its bottom, so all information stays visible.

- **Drag** the lamp to move it.
- **Right-click** the lamp for the Options menu (works in the normal window too).
- A plain click still silences an F12 call.
- **Shift+F** again returns to the normal window.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
