### New in v1.12 – F12 call
Signal the other operators on your band without transmitting.

Turn on **Options ▾ → F12 call – blink S&P stations on my band** on every PC (saved per PC). Then:
- **Press F12 in N1MM** → your call is on. Every other PingPong PC that is **on the same band** and in **S&P** blinks in your color with `F12 · <your station>`. Your own lamp gets a thick border in your color.
- **Press F12 again** → the call is off and the blinking stops (also if your lamp closes, or the other PC changes band or goes to Run).
- N1MM still gets F12 normally; PingPong only watches the key, and only while N1MM is the active window.

If N1MM runs as administrator, run PingPong as administrator too.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp and answer **Yes**, or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**.
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.

All PCs need v1.12 for the F12 call.
