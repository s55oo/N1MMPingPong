### Change in v1.15
**Block my F-keys while another station calls (F12)** now blocks **only N1MM's function keys F1–F11**, and only while the call is active. Enter, Insert, `;`, `'`, Esc and all other keys work normally.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
