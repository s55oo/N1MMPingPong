## PingPong v1.60

**Overview: ready check**
- When the Overview starts, every station's row is **green** (ready) or **dark red** (not ready). It stays that way until the station transmits for the first time. From then on the row is colored as before.
- The new **Check** column shows `✔ OK` or what is wrong:
  - `not heard for …`;
  - `v1.59 – update to v1.60`;
  - `no N1MM data`, or the band/mode isn't supported;
  - `no master` or `two masters`;
  - `keyboard watcher not running`.
- The line above the table says `✔ READY` or `✖ NOT READY (2 of 3)`. The web page shows the same.
- The **Ready check** button starts it again, e.g. before day two.

**Overview: Update all lamps…**
- One button on the master PC asks every lamp to download the newest release and restart. No need to go to each PC.
- It first lists which lamps are not up to date.
- Lamps accept it only from the **master PC**, and only from **v1.60** on. **Update every lamp to v1.60 by hand once**; after that the Overview can do it.

Both `n1mm_lamps.exe` and `pingpong_overview.exe` are updated.
