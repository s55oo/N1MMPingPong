### Diagnostics and a fix for the F12 call
- **Lamps report their state to each other.** With **Test mode** on, `pingpong_test.log` now shows what every other PingPong PC sees and does: its N1MM band/mode and Run/S&P, whether the F12 call and F-key blocking are ticked, whether its keyboard watcher runs, which calls it sees and whether its F-keys are blocked. A line is written whenever that state changes.
- **Fix:** packets that N1MM sends to `127.0.0.1` are now recognised as this PC's own station. Before, such a PC did not know its own band or Run/S&P state, so it could neither blink for a call nor block its F-keys.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
