## PingPong v1.88

**Overview: remove a dead station with a right click**

- Right-click a station that is no longer heard (PC off, crashed or disconnected – grey or red row) → **Remove … from the list**. Its row, its "not heard" alarm and its place in the ready check go away; its statistics stay.
- When that lamp is heard again, the station comes back by itself (in the ready check until it transmits).
- A station that is still heard can't be removed. Logged in the event log as `removed from the list`.
- The release now also carries the operator cheat sheet, `PingPong-cheat-sheet.pdf` (one A4 page – print one per position).
