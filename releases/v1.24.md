### Change in v1.24 – F12 lock is unmistakable
While a PC's F-keys are blocked by an F12 call, its lamp and the wide border **swap colors back and forth**: lamp in the caller's color with a dark border, then dark lamp with a border in the caller's color. Without the lock (blocking option off, silenced or after 40 s) it blinks as before.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
