## PingPong v1.51

**Two-master warning**
- The line under the lamp turns **red** if more than one PC is master:
  - `⚠ TWO MASTERS: RUN1, RUN2 – the lamps don't hear each other (firewall?) or run different versions`: two lamps both act as master, so colors and F12 block times can differ. Use **Options ▾ → Allow PingPong in Windows Firewall…** on both PCs, and update all PCs.
  - `⚠ N1MM master on RUN1 and RUN2 – check N1MM's Network Status`: two N1MMs say they are network master. PingPong still picks one master.
- The warning goes away by itself once it's fixed. With **Diagnostics** on, every change is written to the log.

Update all PCs.
