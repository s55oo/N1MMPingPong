## v1.101 – SO2R: each radio tracked on its own

Thanks to **s53zo** (PR #2, with a real S53M1 capture).

- **SO2R fix:** an idle second radio no longer clears the transmitting radio's lamp early. Each N1MM radio (`RadioNr`) now has its own state under one PC; a new TX on the other radio replaces the old one.
- The radio to show is chosen: transmitting first, then the keyboard/receive focus, then transmit focus, then radio 1. Run/S&P role, ping-pong lock, F12 and urgent/priority calls, and phone tiles follow the right radio. The Overview still shows one row per PC and counts real TX starts only.
- TX band comes from `TXFreq` (split works), with `Freq` as fallback.
- Lost TX packets: the default `--stale` is now **15 s** (N1MM sends every 10 s per radio), so long transmissions stay visible and a lost "RX" expires.
- 26 new SO2R tests. Not tested on real SO2R hardware – please try and report.
