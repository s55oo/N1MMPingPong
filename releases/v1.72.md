## PingPong v1.72

**Lamp**
- **Options ▾ → Lamp size:** normal, large or extra large (×1.4 / ×1.8). The lamp, its texts and the line under it grow, e.g. for an operator sitting further from the screen.
- **Options ▾ → Night mode (dim lamp colors):** every lamp color at about half brightness, so a flashing lamp doesn't dazzle at night.
- Both are saved per PC.

**Overview**
- **Test all lamps:** every lamp flashes its own name and role for 3 s (purple/white, e.g. `INB2 · INB`). You see that every PC is reachable and which lamp is which. Nothing is transmitted or blocked.
- **Event log:** a contest timeline in `pingpong_events.csv` next to the exe (opens in Excel). It records:
  - lamps on, restarted, not heard, heard again;
  - F12 and urgent calls;
  - ping-pong WAIT and free;
  - master and ping-pong setting changes;
  - commands sent from the Overview.

  **Open event log** is on the Activity tab.

Update: **Update all lamps…** in the Overview; the Overview updates itself.
