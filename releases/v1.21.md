### Change in v1.21 – useful footer
The line under the lamp now shows every station's **band, mode and Run/S&P** instead of packet counts, this PC first:

`14 CW Run (ti) · OO840G3 14 CW S&P · OO840G5 7 SSB Run`

While someone transmits: `OO840G3 oddaja · 14 CW S&P`. Each PC is listed once, and stations not heard for a minute drop off.

### Updating
- **From v1.6 or newer:** start the lamp (or **Options ▾ → Check for updates**).
- **From v1.5:** download `n1mm_lamps.exe` below and replace the old one.
