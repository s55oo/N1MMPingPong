## v1.100 – RTTY-R and phone modes (USB/LSB)

- **RTTY-R** (reverse RTTY, as N1MM reports it) now counts as RTTY, so lamps match and no longer show "no band/mode". Thanks to **s53zo** for PR #1 and the diagnostics.
- **USB, LSB and SSB** are one phone group (already so), and now the mode text is matched ignoring case and spaces – `usb`, ` Lsb ` also work. An LSB radio and a USB radio on the same band share one lamp group.
- New tests for USB/LSB/SSB and RTTY-R.
