## PingPong v1.65

**Ping-pong lock on/off: on the master**
- New on the master: **Options ▾ → Ping-pong lock (RUN first, inband stations take turns) – for all PCs**. It is on by default.
- **Untick it and nobody is locked.** Every station may transmit without priority. The setting reaches all lamps at once.
- Other PCs show it greyed out, e.g. `Ping-pong lock: OFF – everybody may transmit (set on master RUN1)`.
- The Overview shows it in its top line: `ping-pong lock: ON / OFF`.
- A new master keeps the current setting.

Update all PCs (**Update all lamps…** in the Overview). Older lamps ignore the switch and keep locking.
