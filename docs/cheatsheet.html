2 · Your keys

F12 in S&PThe S&P stations on your band blink in your color and hold off (call time, default 30 s). F12 again = end the call.
F12 in RunAll stations on your band hold off for a few seconds (default 5 s).
Shift+F2 / F4N1MM sends F2/F4 as usual, and every lamp on your band and mode flashes your name and the message while it is sent.
Run / S&PYour role follows N1MM: Run = RUN, S&P = INB – nothing to set.
MouseLeft click on lamp = stop blinking · right click = Options · Shift+F = compact lamp.

"F12 busy · RUN2" = a call is already running on your band; wait until it ends.

3 · Taking turns (ping-pong)

RUN transmits→GO→first INB takes slot→other INBs WAIT→RUN transmits
  • The slot also frees itself after the release time (set on the master, default 40 s; 0 = only RUN frees it).
  • The master can switch the lock off – then everybody may transmit, no priority.
  • no RUN on 7 MHz – lock off: there is no RUN on your band, so nobody is locked.
  • Only transmissions N1MM starts count. Paddle, microphone PTT or footswitch are not seen and not blocked.

Phone lamp

Options ▾ → Phone lamp QR code… → scan it with the phone. The phone shows the same lamp (needs the firewall rule).
